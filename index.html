Everything a technical notebook needs

Organized like a notebook, fast like a text editor.

Free-form pages

Click anywhere to type. Drag boxes around, resize them, color them.

Rich text, Markdown shortcuts

Headings, colors, highlights and fonts. Type # , - or [] and it formats as you go.

Real tables

Cell colors, borders, merged cells and header rows, with a dedicated table toolbar.

Code blocks

Syntax highlighting for Bash, SQL, Python, YAML, JSON, PowerShell and more. One-click copy; long commands scroll or wrap.

Screenshots

Paste screenshots straight in and resize them by dragging a corner.

Templates

Runbook, incident review, change request, troubleshooting, meeting notes, daily log, or your own.

Instant search

Full-text search across every notebook as you type, plus find-in-page.

Never lose work

Auto-save, page history with restore, and Ctrl+Z for deleted boxes and pages.

Export that keeps the look

A page or a whole notebook to one HTML file, or to PDF.

Coming from OneNote?

Import OneNote sections with their layout, colors and images. Your pages keep their place.

Linked notes

Type [[ to link pages. See what links here, and jump anywhere with Ctrl+P.

PDF beside your notes

Open a vendor guide or a runbook PDF next to the page, and copy text straight into your notes.

Reminders

Put a date on any checklist item. Repeat it daily, weekly or monthly, and get a desktop notification.

Secret pages

Lock a page with a password. It is encrypted on disk (AES-256) and hidden from search.

See it in action

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Pages are files in your Documents folder: back them up, sync them with any tool, or take them with you.
