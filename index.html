Everything a technical notebook needs

Organized like a notebook, fast like a text editor.

Write it your way

Free-form pages

Click anywhere to type. Drag boxes around, resize and color them. Every page has its own title and date, like in OneNote.

Rich text and screenshots

Headings, colors, highlights and fonts; paste screenshots and resize them. Type # , - or [] and it formats as you go.

Real tables

Cell colors, borders, merged cells and header rows, with a dedicated table toolbar.

Code blocks

Syntax highlighting for Bash, SQL, Python, YAML, JSON, PowerShell and more. One-click copy; long commands scroll or wrap.

Handwriting

Write and draw on any page with a pen, mouse or finger: pressure-sensitive pen, highlighter in five colors, and an eraser.

Record audio

Record a meeting or lecture right into the page while you keep typing your notes.

Organize everything

Notebooks, chapters, sections

A real hierarchy, like OneNote. Drag and drop or use Move to… to reorganize pages, sections and chapters; color-code them.

Templates

Runbook, incident review, change request, troubleshooting, meeting notes, daily log, or your own.

Linked notes

Type [[ to link pages. See what links here, jump anywhere with Ctrl+P, and export the link graph.

Reminders

Put a date on any checklist item, repeat it daily, weekly or monthly, and get a desktop notification.

PDF beside your notes

Open a vendor guide or runbook PDF next to the page, and copy text straight into your notes.

Coming from OneNote?

Import whole notebooks straight from the OneNote app on Windows, with sections, layout, colors and images, or from .mht exports.

Find it, keep it

Instant search

Full-text search across every notebook as you type, plus find-in-page.

Never lose work

Auto-save, page history with restore, and Ctrl+Z for deleted boxes and pages.

Export that keeps the look

A page or a whole notebook to one HTML file, or to PDF, handwriting included.

Private, and in sync

Sync between your computers

Optional. Notes are encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know before they're uploaded, so the server can't read them. Conflicts never lose text.

Secret pages and locked sections

Lock a page, or a whole section or chapter, with a password. Encrypted on disk (AES-256) and hidden from search until you unlock.

Updates in one click

New versions install from inside the app. Every update is signed and checked before it's installed.
