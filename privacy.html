Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Qalam Notes is a note-taking app that runs entirely on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. Qalam Notes does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.

Where your data is stored

  • Your notes, images and templates are stored as files in a folder on your computer (by default Documents\Qalam Notes). You can change this folder in Settings.
  • Settings, the search index and page history are stored in the app's data folder on your computer.
  • None of this leaves your computer unless you copy, sync or export it yourself.

Network access

Qalam Notes works offline. It only opens links in your web browser when you choose to open them, and opens exported files in your browser when you choose “Export as PDF”.

Exports

When you export a page or notebook, the file is saved where you choose. Sharing it is up to you.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. When you download the app, the file is served by GitHub, and the page asks GitHub's public API for the latest version number; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Future features

If optional features such as sync are added, they will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated before they are released.
