Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Qalam Notes is a note-taking app that runs entirely on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. Qalam Notes has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising, and never sends your notes anywhere.

The only exception is feedback you choose to send (from the app's “Send feedback” form or the form on this website). It contains what you type: the type of message, an optional star rating, your message, and optionally a name and an email address. Feedback sent from the app also includes the app version and operating system (for example “0.8.1, Windows”), shown in the form before you send it.

  • Feedback is stored in a Google Sheet owned by the developer (Google's privacy policy applies to that storage) and is only used to improve Qalam Notes and to reply to you.
  • A review is shown on this website only if you tick “Show my review” and after the developer approves it. Only your name (as you typed it), rating, review text and the month are shown; your email address is never shown.
  • To have your feedback or review removed, email the address below.

Where your data is stored

  • Your notes, images and templates are stored as files in a folder on your computer (by default Documents\Qalam Notes). You can change this folder in Settings.
  • Settings, the search index and page history are stored in the app's data folder on your computer.
  • None of this leaves your computer unless you copy, sync or export it yourself.

Network access

Qalam Notes works offline. It connects to the internet only to: check GitHub for a new version (at start, can be turned off in Settings), send feedback when you press Send, and open links or exported files in your browser when you choose to.

Exports

When you export a page or notebook, the file is saved where you choose. Sharing it is up to you.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. When you download the app, the file is served by GitHub, and the page asks GitHub's public API for the latest version number; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to those requests. The reviews section and the feedback form talk to the Google Apps Script described above.

Sync (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on. When you use it:

  • Your notes are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your sync passphrase before they are uploaded (end-to-end encryption). The sync server stores only encrypted data, random IDs, file sizes and times. It cannot read your notes, their titles or their folder names, and neither can the developer.
  • The server stores your account: your email address, a hash of your password (or the link to your Google or Microsoft account if you sign in with one), and whether your email is verified. It sends verification and password-reset emails through an email provider.
  • The sync server is run by the developer (Romman Tech). Deleting your account removes all your synced data from it; email the address below.
